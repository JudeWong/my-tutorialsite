# Battle Arena character assets

The current arena uses the textured CC0 armored characters and scanned
industrial scenery in `apocalypse/`. See `apocalypse/CHARACTERS.md` and
`apocalypse/ENVIRONMENT.md` for source links, licensing and conversion details.
The older models below remain for compatibility but are not rendered in the
current arena.

`kaykit-knight.glb` and `kaykit-mage.glb` are KayKit Adventurers 2.0 assets
distributed under CC0-1.0. They were sourced from the public collection at
<https://github.com/GeorgeQLe/assets-kaykit-3d-characters> and are used as
lightweight, web-ready GLB characters in the arena. Credit to Kay Lousberg is
appreciated but not required by the license.
